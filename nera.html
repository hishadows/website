— The idea that started everything

"You deserve a bikini that holds you up and lasts a lifetime — not one you have to hold together."

Co-founder
Liz Assimes

I spent years buying size large tops to fit my cup size, only to end up with a band that would swim up on me and a bikini that never quite looked the way it should.

When I looked for swimwear online, I was faced with the inevitable dilemma: pick a cute set, or choose the bottom coverage I actually wanted. I kept thinking — why don't swim companies offer more variation?

The answer became NERA. The idea took shape in my third year of university, when my field hockey team became our first — and very honest — focus group. Not one of them had a swim brand they loved.

Off the clock

Eight cities lived in — Toronto, Montréal, Barcelona, Cape Town, Athens, Melbourne, Vancouver, and Andover. The Habs and Drake, forever. Consignment shopping, brand strategy books, heated Pilates.

@elizabeth.assimes ↗

"If you can order a bra online with complete confidence — why not a bikini?"

Co-founder
Miranda Badovinac

I love to travel and procrastinate, so my vacation haul usually happens days before the trip. Still, that has never stopped me from placing an online order and trusting the optimistic end of the estimated shipping window.

I've become a bit of an expert on reading a size guide. But a shirt can be a little tight, jorts a little loose — in a bikini, a sizing mismatch can be lethal.

Meanwhile, I've worn the same bra size since the end of puberty, and I can order a bra online with complete confidence. So why couldn't bikinis work the same way? That's precisely what NERA is all about.

Off the clock

Raised in rural Burlington, Ontario — six chickens — converted city girl by U of T. Stanley Park tanning, I Don't Run Club, and an iced vanilla latte from De Mello, always.

@mirandabadovinac ↗
