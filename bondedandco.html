Our Story

Started by two friends who loved the idea of never taking it off.

Bonded & Co. started small — two friends who got their own permanent bracelets welded on and couldn't stop talking about it. What began as something fun to share turned into a full pop-up: bracelets, anklets, necklaces and rings, welded closed right on you, no clasps required. Now we bring that same experience to parties, markets, and pop-ups across Windsor and Essex County.

The Process
